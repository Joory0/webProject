What is WePlay?

Saudi esports is growing fast, but match information is spread across many accounts. Fans often miss their team's match because they did not know the time or the game.

WePlay collects the matches of Saudi esports clubs in one simple website, so fans can see the schedule in seconds.

Why Use WePlay?

  • One schedule for all Saudi clubs.
  • Clear match times so you never miss a game.
  • Information about each club and its games.

How It Works

  1. Create a free account from the Sign Up page.
  2. Check the upcoming matches table.
  3. Follow your favorite clubs.
