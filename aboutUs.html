Our Story

We are students at King Abdulaziz University who love esports. We noticed that Saudi fans had to check many accounts just to know when their team plays, so we built WePlay.

Our Goal

Make following Saudi esports as easy as checking one page.

What We Offer

  • Match schedules
    • Date and time in KSA time
    • Game and tournament name
  • Club information
  • A free account to follow your clubs
